Features

Everything for juggling many accounts — nothing you don't need

Built like a modern desktop app: fast, keyboard-friendly, and honest about what it knows.

One-click, signed-in launch

Pick a game — Place ID, game link, private server or a specific server — and open Roblox already signed in as the right account.

Launch Together

Select several accounts and open them side by side. Multi Roblox runs them in parallel, one window at a time, with live progress and a cancel button.

Unlimited, organized accounts

Nicknames, groups, tags, notes, favorites and pins. Search, filter and sort; switch between cards and a compact table.

Encrypted sign-ins

Sessions are encrypted with Windows' own data protection, kept apart from everything else, and never written to logs or backups.

Honest live status

Online / in-game status straight from Roblox, batched into one request and always labelled with how fresh it is. Never guessed.

Command palette

Press Ctrl K, type a name, hit Enter — you're launching. Shortcuts for everything else, too.

Saved games & servers

Keep your favorite games and private servers one click away. Recent games are remembered automatically.

Backups, themes & updates

Export your organization (never your sign-ins), choose from 8 themes including light, and get verified updates automatically.

Install

Up and running in about a minute

    Download

    Get the installer from the latest release. No account or payment needed.

  2. 2

    Run the installer

    It installs just for you — no administrator rights. Windows may show “Windows protected your PC” because the app isn't code-signed: click More info → Run anyway.

  3. 3

    Add your accounts

    Choose Sign in with Roblox and log in on Roblox's own page. The account appears automatically — nothing to copy.

    Pick a game & launch

    Enter a Place ID or paste a game link, then hit Launch — or select several accounts and Launch together.

Requirements

  • Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit
  • Roblox installed (Bloxstrap and Fishstrap work too)
  • About 300 MB of disk space

Prefer no installer?

The portable .exe runs from anywhere, like a USB stick. It doesn't update itself — grab new versions here.

Updates & uninstall

The installed app checks for updates and asks before installing them. Uninstall from Windows Settings → Apps; your data folder is kept unless you delete it.

Privacy & security

Your accounts never leave your PC

Roblox Account Manager has no servers. It talks only to Roblox (to show profiles and launch games) and to GitHub (to check for updates).

  • Sign-ins are encrypted with Windows DPAPI, tied to your Windows user.
  • Sign-ins are never written to logs, diagnostic reports or backups.
  • The UI runs sandboxed with a strict content security policy.
  • Removing an account deletes its saved sign-in, notes and cached images.
  • Every line of code is public on GitHub.
FAQ

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. There's no paid version, no subscription, no ads and no in-app purchases. It's open source under the MIT license.

Is it safe to give the app my Roblox sign-in?

The recommended way is “Sign in with Roblox”, which uses Roblox's own login page in a private window. The resulting session is encrypted with Windows' built-in protection and only ever sent to Roblox. Never share a .ROBLOSECURITY cookie with anyone — including people offering “help”.

Can I run several accounts at the same time?

Yes — turn on Multi Roblox in Settings, close Roblox, then use Launch Together. Each account opens in its own Roblox window.

Does it work with Bloxstrap or Fishstrap?

Yes. By default the app uses whichever launcher Windows has registered for Roblox, and you can pick one explicitly in Settings.

Can this get my account banned?

The app uses the same sign-in tickets as Roblox's own Play button. However, Roblox doesn't officially support running several clients at once, and using alternate accounts to break Roblox's Terms of Use is against their rules. Use it responsibly — at your own risk.

Is there a Mac or Linux version?

Not currently. Launching Roblox relies on Windows, so the app targets Windows 10 and 11.

Is this made by Roblox?

No. It's an independent community project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Roblox Corporation.
